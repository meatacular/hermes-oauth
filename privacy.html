Hermes privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Richard Culph. When the owner signs in with Google, Hermes receives access only to the owner's own account data for the scopes shown on the consent screen.

What is accessed and why

Email, calendar, files, documents, spreadsheets and contacts are read or changed only to carry out tasks the owner asks for.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens and any data Hermes handles stay on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
